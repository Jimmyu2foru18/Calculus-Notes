## Key Formulas at a Glance

1. $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+2x-3}{x^2-9}$
2. Factor $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+3)}$
3. Removable Discontinuity at $x=-3$
4. Infinite discontinuity at $x=3$
5. $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+9x+22}{x^2-10x+25}$
6. $f(x)=\dfrac{(x+1)(x+5)}{(x-5)(x+5)}$
7. $x+5=0 \Rightarrow x=-5$ — Removable discontinuity (hole) at $x=-5$
8. $x-5=0 \Rightarrow x=5$ — Infinite discontinuity at $x=5$
9. $f(x)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{3x-2}{x^2-4x+4}, & x<0 \text{ — Rational function} \\ x^2-4x+4, & 0 \le x \le 3 \text{ = Polynomial} \\ \dfrac{-7}{(x+4)(x-4)}, & x > 4 \text{ — Rational function} \end{cases}$
10. At $x=0$ — Bl(1), $-0.5$; Bl(2), $4$; Jump discontinuity at $x=0$
11. At $x=3$ — LHL, $1$; $f(3)$, $1$; Infinite discontinuity at $x=4$
12. $f(x)=\dfrac{|x-1|}{x^2-1}$
13. discontinuity at $x=1$ = undefined; Bl(1), $\dfrac{1}{1+1}=\dfrac{1}{2}$
