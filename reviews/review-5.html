Review: Advanced Derivatives

This sheet covers derivatives of exponential and logarithmic functions, inverse trigonometric derivatives, higher-order derivatives, and related rates formulas.

Derivatives of Exponential Functions

The natural exponential function is unique: its derivative equals itself.

\[ \frac{d}{dx}[e^x] = e^x \]
Derivative of $e^x$
\[ \frac{d}{dx}[a^x] = a^x \ln a \quad \text{(for } a > 0, a \neq 1\text{)} \]
Derivative of $a^x$

Key fact: $e$ is the only base for which $\dfrac{d}{dx}[a^x] = a^x$ at $x = 0$.

Derivatives of Logarithmic Functions

Natural logs and general logs follow from the inverse relationship with $e^x$:

\[ \frac{d}{dx}[\ln x] = \frac{1}{x}, \quad x > 0 \]
Derivative of natural log
\[ \frac{d}{dx}[\log_a x] = \frac{1}{x \ln a}, \quad x > 0 \]
Derivative of general log

Domain: $\ln x$ and $\log_a x$ are only defined for $x > 0$. The derivative formula $\frac{1}{x}$ inherits this restriction.

Logarithmic Differentiation Recap

For $y = f(x)^{g(x)}$ or complicated products, take $\ln$ first:

\[ \ln y = g(x) \ln f(x) \implies \frac{1}{y}\frac{dy}{dx} = g'(x)\ln f(x) + g(x)\frac{f'(x)}{f(x)} \]
Logarithmic differentiation template

Inverse Trig Derivatives (Review)

These are restated here for quick reference. They are derived using implicit differentiation.

\[ \frac{d}{dx}[\sin^{-1}(x)] = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}, \quad |x| < 1 \]
Arcsine
\[ \frac{d}{dx}[\cos^{-1}(x)] = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}, \quad |x| < 1 \]
Arccosine
\[ \frac{d}{dx}[\tan^{-1}(x)] = \frac{1}{1+x^2} \]
Arctangent

Absolute value matters: For $\sec^{-1}$ and $\csc^{-1}$, the denominator is $|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}$, not just $x\sqrt{x^2-1}$.

Worked Example: Inverse Trig with Chain Rule

Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sec^{-1}(5x^3)]$:

\[ f'(x) = \frac{1}{|5x^3|\sqrt{(5x^3)^2 - 1}} \cdot 15x^2 = \frac{15x^2}{5x^3\sqrt{25x^6 - 1}} = \frac{3}{x\sqrt{25x^6 - 1}} \]
Chain rule + arcsecant derivative

Higher-Order Derivatives

The second derivative is the derivative of the first derivative. Higher-order derivatives follow similarly.

\[ f''(x) = \frac{d}{dx}[f'(x)] = \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \]
Second derivative notation
\[ f^{(n)}(x) = \frac{d^n}{dx^n}[f(x)] \]
$n$-th derivative notation

Worked Examples: Higher-Order Derivatives

Find $f^{(\text{IV})}(x)$ for $f(x) = 3x^6 + 5x^5 - 3x^4 + 3x^3 - 9x^2 + 10x - 4$:

\[ f^{(IV)}(x) = 1080x^2 + 60x - 72 \]
Fourth derivative of polynomial

Find $f^{(\text{IV})}(x)$ for $f(x) = \cos(2x)$:

\[ f'(x) = -2\sin(2x), \quad f''(x) = -4\cos(2x), \quad f'''(x) = 8\sin(2x), \quad f^{(IV)}(x) = 16\cos(2x) \]
Fourth derivative of $\cos(2x)$

Pattern for trig: Derivatives of $\sin$ and $\cos$ cycle every 4 derivatives. For $\cos(kx)$, each derivative brings out a factor of $k$.

Related Rates Method

To solve related rates problems:

  1. Draw a diagram and label all quantities that change with time.
  2. Write an equation relating the variables (geometry, physics).
  3. Differentiate both sides with respect to $t$.
  4. Substitute known values (including rates) and solve for the unknown rate.

Critical rule: Differentiate first, then substitute. If you substitute before differentiating, constant values may hide the time-dependence of other variables.

Worked Example: Airplane and Radar

An airplane flies at constant height 6 km toward a radar station. If the distance $s$ decreases at 400 km/hr when $s = 10$ km, find the horizontal speed.

\[ x^2 + 6^2 = s^2 \implies 2x\frac{dx}{dt} = 2s\frac{ds}{dt} \implies \frac{dx}{dt} = \frac{s}{x}\frac{ds}{dt} = \frac{10}{8}(-400) = -500 \text{ km/hr} \]
Horizontal speed = 500 km/hr toward the station

Worked Example: Expanding Circle

If the radius of a circle increases at 2 cm/s, how fast is the area increasing when $r = 5$ cm?

\[ A = \pi r^2 \implies \frac{dA}{dt} = 2\pi r \frac{dr}{dt} = 2\pi(5)(2) = 20\pi \text{ cm}^2/\text{s} \]
Rate of area change

Practice Problems

  1. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[e^{3x^2}]$ using the chain rule.
  2. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[5^x]$ using the exponential rule.
  3. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[\ln(x^3 + 1)]$ using the chain rule.
  4. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[\log_2(x^2 + 3)]$ using the change-of-base formula.
  5. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sin^{-1}(2x)]$ and state the domain restriction.
  6. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}[\tan^{-1}(x^2)]$ using the chain rule.
  7. Find $f''(x)$ for $f(x) = x^3 e^x$.
  8. Find $f'''(x)$ for $f(x) = \ln(x^2 + 1)$.
  9. A spherical balloon is inflated at $10 \text{ cm}^3/\text{s}$. How fast is the radius increasing when $r = 5$ cm?
  10. A 10 ft ladder leans against a wall. If the bottom slides away at 1 ft/s, how fast is the top sliding down when the bottom is 6 ft from the wall?
